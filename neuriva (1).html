01 — The Story

Why we built NEURIVA

Stress is everywhere — in late-night study sessions, back-to-back deadlines, and the quiet weight of uncertainty. But our bodies have been silently sending distress signals long before we consciously feel overwhelmed.

NEURIVA was born from a simple question: what if a device could detect your stress in real time, understand what your calm looks like, and then gently stimulate your nervous system back to it?

We built a closed-loop system that reads your body's electrical language — heart rhythms, sweat responses, brainwaves — and responds with targeted vagal nerve stimulation to restore balance. No medication. No side effects. Just you and your own biology, in sync.

02 — The Science

How we read your stress

💧

GSR

Galvanic Skin Response

Your skin conducts electricity differently depending on how much you sweat. When you're stressed, your sympathetic nervous system activates sweat glands — especially on palms and fingertips — spiking skin conductance in milliseconds. NEURIVA reads this micro-sweating as a precise, real-time stress marker that doesn't lie.

Sympathetic activation sensor
💓

ECG

Electrocardiography & HRV

Your heart doesn't beat like a metronome — it varies subtly with every breath and emotion. This Heart Rate Variability (HRV) is a powerful window into autonomic nervous system balance. Low HRV signals high stress. NEURIVA analyzes your unique HRV patterns to detect distress states you might not consciously feel yet.

Heart rate variability analysis
⚡

EEG

Brainwaves & Frequency Bands

Your brain generates electrical oscillations in distinct frequency bands: Delta (deep sleep), Theta (drowsy), Alpha (calm focus), Beta (active thinking), and Gamma (high cognition). Stress pushes the brain toward high Beta and suppressed Alpha. NEURIVA monitors these band ratios to map your cognitive-emotional state in real time.

Alpha/Beta band ratio monitoring
🤖

Personalized ML

Baseline-Calibrated Machine Learning

Stress is deeply personal — your calm looks different from everyone else's. NEURIVA begins with a calibration phase to capture your unique physiological baseline across all three sensors. A personalized ML model then learns the delta between your resting state and your stress state, enabling highly accurate detection tuned to your individual biology — not a population average.

Per-user baseline calibration

03 — The Relief

Restoring calm with tVNS

Once NEURIVA detects a stress event, it activates Transcutaneous Vagus Nerve Stimulation — a non-invasive technique that delivers gentle electrical pulses to the auricular branch of the vagus nerve, accessible through the ear.

The vagus nerve is the body's master regulator of the parasympathetic nervous system — the "rest and digest" counterpart to fight-or-flight. Stimulating it directly activates a calming cascade: heart rate slows, breathing deepens, cortisol drops, and rational prefrontal cortex activity is restored.

tVNS Activated

03

Parasympathetic Response

Vagal signal travels brainstem → nucleus tractus solitarius → cortex. HRV increases, Alpha waves rise, calm restored.

04

Closed Loop Complete

System continuously monitors recovery. Stimulation adjusts or terminates based on real-time biometric feedback.
